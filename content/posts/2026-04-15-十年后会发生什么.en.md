Imagining what the world will be like ten years from now is an interesting thing, especially when you are in an industry undergoing such dramatic changes, making the future even more anticipated. Here, I would like to briefly share some personal opinions and then let time verify the answers.

**Opinion One:** The number of practitioners in the software engineering industry will increase dramatically, and market competition will become incredibly fierce. In the past, this work was seen as a high-barrier technical profession, where only a few practitioners could enjoy the benefits. Now, with the support of large models, the cost of programming has been crushed to almost zero, and anyone can easily become a top programmer through large models. Natural language is the ultimate form of programming language. Then, those who got on board early and enjoyed the benefits due to the times will no longer exist. Once the entry barrier of an industry is low enough, it will bring tremendous competitive pressure.

**Opinion Two:** The form of human-computer interaction will undergo a major change, and the terminal market may see new dark horse companies emerge. This is a naturally deduced result: since the cost of programming approaches zero infinitely, could there be a new form of human-computer interaction, where each user has their own personalized interactive experience? This new form is exciting just to think about.

**Opinion Three:** A large amount of work will disappear due to AI automation, and the value of repetitive work will become lower and lower. Humans need to rethink the value of work. New demands, new enterprises, and new positions will emerge here. But what exactly they will be, I cannot yet see.

**Opinion Four:** The capabilities of large models will penetrate all industries, increasingly becoming the underlying infrastructure of the entire society. Everyone will be inseparable from AI. The essence of AI is an accelerator for the social engine, enabling everyone to build upon the work of predecessors more quickly and accelerating the operation of the entire society. And here, the most core issue is how to explain the mechanism of intelligence emergence.
