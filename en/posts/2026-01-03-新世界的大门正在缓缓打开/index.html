A New World

January 3, 2026 · 1 min · Honghui Liu | Translations:
  • 中文

It took me two hours to rebuild my personal blog, leveraging the power of the currently popular AI IDE (TRAE), which was unimaginable in the past.

AI has lowered the barrier to programming to an unprecedented level of simplicity. Complex programming tasks can now be accomplished using only natural language.

The most promising directions I see for large models are code and mathematics. This change is revolutionary.

Many of our established ways of thinking need to change.

The traditional programming paradigm is an inefficient but human-certifiable correct approach, whereas now we need to shift our thinking. AI programming is an efficient, concurrent but probabilistically error-prone approach. From the perspective of human social development, the latter is superior.

There is one more important problem that needs to be solved: the extremely inefficient communication between people.

If the brain-computer interface project can be realized, humanity may truly enter a completely new society.
