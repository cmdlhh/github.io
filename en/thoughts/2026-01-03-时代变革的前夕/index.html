The Eve of Era Transformation

January 3, 2026 · 1 min · Honghui Liu | Translations:
  • 中文

We are standing on the eve of a massive era transformation. Every time I think about this, I get incredibly excited. The core element of all this is AI, especially the powerful capabilities of current LLMs. I still position it as a productivity tool, but the capabilities this tool demonstrates are like human intelligence, and in some sense have far exceeded humans. This dimension of transcendence is something that no previous productivity revolution has encountered. What will happen in the future? I’m really looking forward to it.
